A futuristic AI learning platform combining chat, study mode, viva exams, and PDF export into one installable PWA — built for IELTS & BCS prep and beyond.

  • AI Engine — Multi-key fallback (OpenRouter → Gemini Direct → Cloudflare AI Workers → Pollinations.ai) so it always has a working brain
  • AI Compare Mode — Run the same question through multiple models side-by-side
  • Online / Offline toggle — Built-in knowledge base + rule engine keeps it fully usable offline
  • Study Mode — AI flashcards (text, image, or PDF → cards with flip animation + hints)
  • Quiz — Multiple-choice with difficulty levels and AI re-explanation of wrong answers
  • Spaced Repetition (SRS) — SM-2 algorithm with daily streaks and due badges
  • Summarizer — Paste any text → bullets / paragraph / ELI5 / key terms / TL;DR, or compare two models
  • Viva Mode — AI oral-exam simulator: question-by-question, scored critique, full grade report
  • Progress Dashboard — Bar chart of study time, quiz accuracy sparkline, SRS progress, streak
  • PDF Export — Print-ready SRS decks, quiz history stats table, Viva results with per-question feedback
  • PWA — Installable on Android, iOS, desktop; offline service worker (cache-first assets, network-first app shell); Cloudflare AI Worker proxy for free, CORS-safe inference
  • Voice — YouTube-style flashcard & summary playback · ElevenLabs voice mode · IELTS 4-part preparation · BCS preparation track
